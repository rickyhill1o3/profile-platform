How it works

  1. Sign in, open an available raffle, and click Enter raffle before its countdown ends.
  2. All-member raffles accept every active website account: users, admins, and the super admin. Private group raffles accept only members in the selected admin group.
  3. When the countdown reaches zero, the server locks the manual entries and randomly draws one winner.
  4. The winner receives a private email with a direct checkout link and also sees checkout here.
  5. Retail raffles require payment before the claim deadline; free-prize raffles require no payment.

Raffles are not available to public shop visitors. Creating an account does not automatically enter a raffle. Odds depend on the number of members who manually enter. If a retail winner does not complete checkout by the deadline, the same locked entry pool can be used for a fallback redraw.
